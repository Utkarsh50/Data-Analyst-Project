# Orders by Time of Day

**Query:** [`../SQL Queries/07_time_of_day_orders.sql`](../SQL%20Queries/07_time_of_day_orders.sql)

## Output

| time_of_the_day | total_orders |
|---|---|
| Dawn (0–6h) | 5242 |
| Morning (7–12h) | 27733 |
| Night (19–23h) | 28331 |
| Afternoon (13–18h) | 38135 |

## Insights

Most Brazilian customers place their orders during the afternoon, followed by night, morning, and
then dawn. This indicates customers are generally most active and prefer shopping during afternoon
and evening hours.
