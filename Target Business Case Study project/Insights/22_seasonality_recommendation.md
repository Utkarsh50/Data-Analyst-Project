## Insights

Most orders are made in November, January, and March.

**Recommendation:** Target should make critical logistics and warehouse inventory preparations at
least 3–4 months ahead of these peak sales periods, ensuring sufficient stock is available for high
demand. Target can also increase marketing campaigns and discounts during these windows to
capitalize on naturally higher customer intent and drive further sales growth.
