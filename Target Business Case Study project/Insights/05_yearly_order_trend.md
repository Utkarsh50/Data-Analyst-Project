# Yearly Order Trend

**Query:** [`../SQL Queries/05_yearly_order_trend.sql`](../SQL%20Queries/05_yearly_order_trend.sql)

## Output

| order_year | Total_orders |
|---|---|
| 2016 | 329 |
| 2017 | 45101 |
| 2018 | 54011 |

## Insights

From the start date of September 2016, order count has shown a clear upward trend. In 2017, total
orders approached 45k, while in 2018 there were already approximately 54k orders through October
alone — higher than the entire year of 2017. This clearly shows order volume is increasing
year over year.
