## Insights

States with the highest average delivery time (RR, AP, AM, AL, PA) also tend to have a
comparatively smaller customer base than states with the lowest delivery time (SP, RR, MG, DF,
SC), pointing to a link between logistics reach and customer growth in these regions.

**Recommendation:** Target should prioritize investing in logistics support and warehousing in the
states with the highest average delivery time, to reduce delivery time and improve product
availability — which could also help grow the customer base there.
