# Incident Volume by Contact Type

**Query:** [`../SQL Queries/03_incident_volume_by_contact_type.sql`](../SQL%20Queries/03_incident_volume_by_contact_type.sql)

## Output

| contact_type   | Total_count |
|----------------|-------------|
| Phone          | 24688 |
| Self service   | 158   |
| Email          | 59    |
| IVR            | 9     |
| Direct opening | 4     |

## Insights

Phone accounts for 24,688 of 24,918 incidents (99.1%), with self-service (158), Email (59), IVR
(9), and Direct opening (4) making up the remaining fraction. Incident reporting is almost
entirely funneled through phone contact, with self-service and other digital channels seeing
negligible adoption.
