## Insights

The dataset spans February 2016 to February 2017. Incident volume is heavily concentrated in
March–May 2016 (7,500–9,000/month), then drops sharply to single/low double digits from June 2016
onward, with a small uptick in Jan–Feb 2017 (94, 55).

The data reveals that the majority of incidents occurred between March and May 2016, averaging
approximately 7,500 to 9,000 incidents per month. Following May 2016, incident volume experienced
a sharp drop-off to low single- and double-digit counts. This significant reduction warrants
further investigation with operational teams to determine the root cause of the peak incident
volume during the spring of 2016.
